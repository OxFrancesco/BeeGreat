How it's built

  • Remotion 4.0.526 project in apps/pecu/apps/video. Every frame is React and TypeScript driven by useCurrentFrame().
  • It imports theme/theme.css, theme/clay.css and the bundled Inter and JetBrains Mono. The layout sits on a 960×540 canvas zoomed 2×, so type, radii and clay shadows keep their product values.
  • Motion uses the design system's curves: cubic-bezier(.23,1,.32,1) for entrances, (.77,0,.175,1) for movement, the homepage 480 ms / 60 ms tile stagger, and the route timings from the transaction card.
  • The soundtrack is synthesized by scripts/sound.ts: mallets, pad, bass, kick, typing ticks, bloops and a chime. Picture and sound both read src/timeline.ts, so every cue lands on its frame. Mastered to −16 LUFS, −1.9 dBFS peak.
  • All balances, prices, flows and odds are fictional fixtures.

Notes

  • Rendering is pinned to Chrome's ANGLE backend. The default software backend drew a 1 px red fringe around any clay shadow that was fading in.
  • Remotion is free for individuals and companies of up to three people. A larger company needs a Remotion company license.
