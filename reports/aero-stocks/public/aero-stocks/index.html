Run it

aero tui

aero stocks buy --stock NVDAc --amount 25 --dry-run
aero stocks sell --stock NVDAc --amount 0.1 --dry-run

aero index create --name tech --allocations 'NVDAc=50,AAPLc=50'
aero index rebalance --name tech --cash 100 --dry-run

Indices include all wallet holdings of their named stocks. Overlapping indices share holdings. Only the explicit cash contribution spends existing USDC. Keep an exiting stock at 0% until sold.
