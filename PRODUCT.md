# Product

## Register

product

## Users

BeeGreat is for self-directed people who are juggling several meaningful goals and need help choosing what deserves attention now. They often arrive between meetings or tasks, may prefer to speak instead of type, and expect their context to follow them between mobile and web.

## Product Purpose

BeeGreat turns an intention into one clear next focus. Bee helps the user shape goals, choose a daily Highlight, act on it, and see progress reflected in the Hive. Success means the user leaves each visit knowing what to do next, without rebuilding context or managing a complicated system.

## Brand Personality

Focused, warm, playful. Bee is a calm collaborator with a light touch, not a productivity lecturer. Copy is direct and human; delight comes from the bee, honeycomb, and Hive moments that reinforce progress.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards with dense chrome, nested card grids, and decorative metrics.
- Generic AI chat products whose identity stops at a prompt box and a gradient.
- Marketing copy, buzzwords, or explanatory text that delays the primary task.
- Web-only styling that drifts from the mobile app's visual language or duplicates its assets.

## Design Principles

1. Start with the next action. Every screen should make its primary job clear within a glance.
2. Keep one Hive across devices. Auth, agent behavior, data, language, and visual identity must feel continuous.
3. Earn every layer. Add containers, labels, and controls only when they clarify structure or state.
4. Let progress feel alive. Reserve personality and motion for voice, completion, and Hive feedback.
5. Make recovery ordinary. Voice denial, network failures, and agent uncertainty should always preserve a clear text path.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for web. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, screen-reader names and live regions, sufficient contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced motion. Never rely on color alone for state. Voice is optional: microphone denial, transcription failure, and unavailable speech must retain an equivalent typed workflow.
