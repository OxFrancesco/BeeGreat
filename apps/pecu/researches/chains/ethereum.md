- Ethereum TVL is mostly ETH and liquid staking tokens, so it tracks the ETH price closely. Measure the price effect before crediting deposits.
- Restaking, liquid staking and lending protocols hold most of the TVL. Large moves often come from a single protocol's deposits or withdrawals.
- Network upgrades, blob fee changes and L2 activity change mainnet fees; check the date of any fork against fee moves.
