# BeeGreat Focus

BeeGreat is a voice-first focus system that helps a person direct attention toward a deliberately small set of meaningful outcomes.

## Focus Structure

**Goal**:
A meaningful long-horizon outcome that gives its Projects a shared purpose and is represented by one GolieBee. Renaming or clarifying its wording preserves the Goal; replacing its intended outcome creates a new Goal.
_Avoid_: Macro-project, habit, highlight

**Active Goal**:
A Goal currently competing for the user's attention. Three is the healthy focus threshold; additional Active Goals remain allowed with Brain Fatigue, up to a hard maximum of seven.
_Avoid_: Goal slot, highlight

**Completed Goal**:
A Goal whose intended outcome the user has explicitly confirmed as achieved. Completing every Project or Task can support that decision but never completes the Goal automatically; its GolieBee graduates to the Hall of Fame.
_Avoid_: Completed project plan, archived Goal, abandoned Goal

**Parked Goal**:
A Goal intentionally removed from active focus without being completed or abandoned. Its Honey and history remain intact, and its sleeping GolieBee returns when the Goal is reactivated.
_Avoid_: Archived Goal, abandoned Goal, deleted Goal

**Abandoned Goal**:
A Goal the user has intentionally ended without completing. Its GolieBee becomes a GhostyBee in the Memorial. Only the Goal's remaining net Honey contribution is removed from the live Hive balance, capped at the current balance so abandonment never creates debt; its full Honey history remains in the ledger. Abandonment can be reversed through Royal-Jelly-powered Resurrection.
_Avoid_: Parked Goal, completed Goal, deleted Goal

**Deleted Goal**:
A Goal erased because it was a mistake or must be removed for privacy. Deletion preserves neither a GolieBee nor a GhostyBee.
_Avoid_: Abandoned Goal, Parked Goal

**Project**:
A finite workstream that advances one Goal by organizing related Tasks.
_Avoid_: Goal, task list

**Task**:
A completable unit of work belonging to one Project.
_Avoid_: Goal, project, action request

**Subtask**:
A smaller completable unit belonging to one Task. A Subtask cannot contain another Subtask.
_Avoid_: Nested task, child project

**Highlight**:
A user's single current, time-boxed pointer to an Active Goal or an unfinished Project or Task that deserves attention now. It owns no work, does not change the focus hierarchy, and expires at the end of its chosen time window. Without a custom duration, it expires at the end of the user's local day; it also expires immediately when its target is no longer actionable.
_Avoid_: Goal, folder, list

## Focus World

**Hive**:
The user's single personal focus space, gathering all Goals, their GolieBees, and the user's Honey. Social comparisons do not create or replace a user's Hive.
_Avoid_: Goal, party, team

**Bee**:
The user's personal voice agent and coordinator inside BeeGreat.
_Avoid_: Goal Bee, project bee, subagent

**GolieBee**:
The living character that represents one Goal inside the user's Hive. Every Goal has exactly one GolieBee, which remains with it when its wording changes and graduates to the Hall of Fame when the Goal is completed.
_Avoid_: Goal Bee, project bee, Bee

**Exhausted GolieBee**:
A GolieBee affected by Brain Fatigue while its Hive has zero Honey. It cannot produce new Honey until the fatigue clears or Genius State is earned.
_Avoid_: Sleeping GolieBee, GhostyBee

**Power-up**:
An optional Hive capability that summons its PowerBee while active. Dismissing it removes the PowerBee's presence without losing the unlocked Power-up or its retained state.
_Avoid_: Goal, permanent Hive member, GolieBee

**PowerBee**:
The non-permanent specialist bee summoned into a Hive by an active Power-up.
_Avoid_: Bee, GolieBee, permanent Hive member

**Booster**:
A temporary gameplay advantage purchased with Royal Jelly. A Booster may summon a PowerBee while its effect lasts, but it is not a retained Power-up.
_Avoid_: Power-up, cosmetic, permanent capability

**Hall of Fame**:
The permanent area of a Hive that preserves GolieBees whose Goals were completed, together with the history of what they achieved.
_Avoid_: Archive, graveyard, deleted goals

**GhostyBee**:
The transformed GolieBee of an Abandoned Goal, preserved as a visible record in the Memorial unless resurrected with Royal Jelly.
_Avoid_: GolieBee, completed Goal, deleted bee

**Memorial**:
The permanent area of a Hive that preserves GhostyBees and the stories of their Abandoned Goals.
_Avoid_: Hall of Fame, archive, trash

## Recognition

**Achievement**:
A permanent, one-time recognition of a meaningful milestone. An Achievement may belong to a ranked series with progressively harder thresholds.
_Avoid_: Repeatable reward, task reward

**Achievement Rank**:
An ordered threshold within an Achievement series, unlocked once when its requirement is met and preserved as its own distinct badge.
_Avoid_: Achievement level, repeat count

**GolieBee Achievement**:
An Achievement earned within one Goal and permanently attached to that Goal's GolieBee.
_Avoid_: Hive Achievement, global badge

**Hive Achievement**:
An Achievement earned across Goals or through the user's overall BeeGreat journey and attached to the Hive.
_Avoid_: GolieBee Achievement, goal badge

## Focus Economy

**Honey**:
The global, spendable, non-negative cosmetic currency belonging to a user's Hive. Progress gains and focus-economy losses are attributed to the Goal that caused them, while cosmetic spending is global and unallocated; Honey loss stops at zero and never creates debt. Honey can unlock expressive Hive and bee customizations but cannot buy gameplay advantages.
_Avoid_: Per-Goal balance, experience points

**Royal Jelly**:
The separate, spendable, non-negative gameplay currency used for boosters, Resurrection, and other in-game advantages. Royal Jelly cannot unlock Goal completion, Achievement ranks, Honey, or Honeycomb Score directly.
_Avoid_: Honey, cosmetic currency, progress score

**Royal Jelly Quest**:
A rolling weekly challenge whose roster is the user's Active Goals when the challenge begins. It rewards balanced verified progress across that fixed roster rather than raw activity volume.
_Avoid_: Login streak, task quota, Genius State

**Resurrection**:
A Royal-Jelly-powered action that restores the original Abandoned Goal directly to Active, transforms its GhostyBee back into a GolieBee, and refunds half the Honey actually removed when the Goal was abandoned. Its full ledger and Achievements remain intact, and Resurrection can immediately affect Brain Fatigue like any other Goal activation.
_Avoid_: Reactivation, recreation, deletion recovery

**Focus Shield**:
A temporary Royal-Jelly Booster that protects one Active Goal from Brain Fatigue while the shield lasts.
_Avoid_: Genius State, permanent protection, cosmetic shield

**Honey Ledger**:
The permanent history of Honey gains and losses, including the Goal responsible for each change.
_Avoid_: Honey balance, GolieBee balance

**Honeycomb Score**:
The non-spendable measure of verified progress and Achievement across the user's BeeGreat history. Spending Honey, abandoning a Goal, and resurrecting a Goal do not change historical Honeycomb Score.
_Avoid_: Honey balance, currency

**Brain Fatigue**:
The Goal-scoped Honey drain associated with having more than three Active Goals. From four through six, its severity follows a bell shape, rising beyond the healthy threshold and then falling toward the exceptional threshold; affected Goals are determined by activation order. Activating the seventh Goal retains the six-Goal penalty until Genius State is earned, when Brain Fatigue disappears completely. At zero Honey it prevents an affected GolieBee from producing more Honey. Brain Fatigue never reduces Honeycomb Score or erases verified progress.
_Avoid_: Goal limit, overage fee

**Genius State**:
The exceptional focus state earned by recording at least one Verified Progress Event on every one of seven Active Goals within a rolling seven-day window. It removes Brain Fatigue completely while qualification is sustained; Goal count alone does not qualify, and losing qualification restores the six-Goal penalty.
_Avoid_: Seven-goal loophole, goal-count bonus

**Verified Progress Event**:
Evidence that an Active Goal moved forward through a completed Task, completed Project, or explicit Goal milestone. These events qualify a seven-Goal Hive for Genius State within its rolling seven-day window.
_Avoid_: App activity, login streak, cosmetic purchase
